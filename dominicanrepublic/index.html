About Us

Welcome to the Dominican Republic Rivian Club — an official chapter of Rivian Clubs of America.

We are a community of Rivian owners and EV enthusiasts based in the Dominican Republic, brought together by a shared passion for adventure, sustainability, and the open road. Whether you’re a longtime Rivian owner or just EV-curious, there’s a place for you here.
