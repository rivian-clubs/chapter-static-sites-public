About Us

The Mid Atlantic Rivian Club is a community for those living in the Mid-Atlantic region — Maryland, Virginia, Washington DC, West Virginia, Pennsylvania, and Delaware — who own, have ordered, or are interested in the Rivian R1T and R1S.

We’re building a community to share photos, stories, plan local meetups, and learn more about our wonderful vehicles. Whether you’re a longtime Rivian owner or just EV-curious, there’s a place for you here.

Join us for events ranging from cars and coffee meetups to trail cleanups and off-road adventures. There’s something for everyone in the Mid Atlantic Rivian Club.
