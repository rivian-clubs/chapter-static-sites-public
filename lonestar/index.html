About Us

Welcome to the Lone Star Rivian Club – the heart of Texas Rivian enthusiasts! As a proud chapter of the Rivian Clubs of America, we unite under the stars to celebrate our shared passion for pioneering adventures, championing the environment, and driving positive change in the world. Join us, and ride the wave of innovation with a community that’s as boundless as the Texan horizon!
